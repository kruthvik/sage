# Library candidates

Mechanisms deliberately kept out of the core loop. Each one needs a criterion before it's offered.

| Candidate | When it could be offered | Source | Why not core |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contrasting cases + "name the invariant" | target is abstraction or changed_application; learner passes familiar cases | D4; R08; I *Transfer* row | Far-transfer gain is conditional on supplying the principle; activity label alone proves nothing |
| Delayed judgement of learning | learner wants calibration feedback | R07 | Predictor, not a mastery signal |
| Representation sequencing (blocked → "increased" interleave) | multiple graphical representations of one idea | D2 (Rau et al. 2010) | One ITS study, fractions, grade 5–6 |
| Generic self-explanation prompts on every example | – | D3; I | Not established; prompt quality matters |
| Adaptive (accuracy/latency) spacing | after the fixed baseline is beaten at equal dose | P3 | Narrow evidence |
